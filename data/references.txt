# Curated Bible references for bible-verse-widget
#
# One reference per line: "<English book name> <chapter>:<verse>[-<verse>]"
# Blank lines and lines starting with "#" are ignored.
#
# The verse TEXT is never stored here. tools/build_verses.py resolves every
# reference against the public-domain source texts and writes data/verses/*.json.
#
# IMPORTANT — versification: German, English and Spanish Bibles disagree on the
# numbering of a handful of chapters. References must only be added from
# chapters that are aligned across all three sources. build_verses.py fails the
# build if a reference cannot be resolved identically everywhere. The known
# divergent chapters, which must be avoided, are:
#   Numbers 12, Numbers 29, 1 Samuel 23, 2 Samuel 20, 2 Chronicles 33,
#   Job 35, Job 38, Job 40, Hosea 11, Jonah (all), Luke 17,
#   Acts 8, Acts 15, Acts 19, Acts 24, Romans 14, Romans 16, 2 Corinthians 13
#
# Equal verse counts are NOT proof of alignment. John 10 has 42 verses in all
# three editions, yet Luther 1912 splits John 10:10 in two and merges 10:11 with
# 10:12, so only verses 10-12 are shifted. build_verses.py therefore also
# compares the length of the three renderings of every reference and fails on a
# large imbalance; that check is what caught John 10.
